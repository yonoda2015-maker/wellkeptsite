Glaze —

Batch of twelve — max 12 per order so the whole firing doesn't leave in one box.

Materials

Mid-fire stoneware clay from Clay Art Center (Tacoma), fired to cone 6 in an electric kiln. Glazes are mixed in-studio from raw materials and tested for stability.

  • Food-safe, lead-free glaze on all eating surfaces
  • Unglazed foot ring, sanded smooth
  • Weight and color vary slightly piece to piece
Care

Dishwasher safe on the top rack, though hand washing keeps the glaze surface brightest. Microwave safe.

  • Avoid thermal shock — don't move from freezer to oven
  • Speckled glazes can mark from metal utensils; a baking-soda paste removes it
  • Not for stovetop or broiler use
Shipping & returns

Ships within 3 business days, double-boxed in paper pulp. Flat $8 shipping, free over $120. If a piece arrives broken we replace it from the next firing. Returns accepted within 30 days — you cover return postage, we refund in full.
