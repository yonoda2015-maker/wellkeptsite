Who's shelving your books

A used bookstore, run like one

Understory opened in 1998 in the basement of a hardware store that has since become three other things. We moved upstairs in 2011 and have stayed put since — same creaky floor, same argument about whether the poetry section should live near the front door (poetry won).

We don't sort by algorithm. Every book that comes through the door is read, or skimmed, or at minimum flipped through by someone on staff before it's priced and shelved. If a book doesn't earn its shelf space, it goes to the discount cart out front instead of the recycling bin — someone always takes it home.

  • 27years open, same neighborhood
  • 6,400+books currently on the shelves
  • 180trade-ins accepted most months
Selling us your books

Trade-ins & buybacks

Bring in a box, a shelf's worth, or a single book you're ready to let go of. We look through everything on the spot — no appointment needed during open hours. You can take store credit (worth 30% more than cash) or cash on the counter.

We're currently buying deep in poetry, story collections, and anything by a writer from this region. We're overstocked on business bestsellers and diet books, so those may get a pass — no hard feelings.

Bring in a box See what we're low on
  • Step 1 We sort on the spotMost boxes are looked through in fifteen minutes or less while you browse.
  • Step 2 You get an offerPriced per book, with the reasoning — condition, demand, how many copies we already have.
  • Step 3 Credit or cashStore credit doesn't expire. It's the better deal, but the choice is yours.

Reading nights & small events

We hold twenty folding chairs and a bad microphone. That's turned out to be enough for a decade of these.

  • First Tue. Understory Reading SeriesTwo local writers, twelve minutes each, then questions until we run out of them.
  • Every Sat. Morning Trade-In HoursExtra staff on the counter 9–11am specifically for boxes of books — shorter wait than weekday afternoons.
  • Last Thu. Quiet Book ClubWe pick something short. Nobody has to have finished it to show up.
  • Seasonal Sidewalk Dollar Cart SaleThe overflow gets a table on the sidewalk and a coffee can for change. First come, first served.
