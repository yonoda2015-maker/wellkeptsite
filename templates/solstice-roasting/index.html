How We Roast

From green seed to your cup, in three steps.

01

Select

We cup every incoming lot blind before it's approved. Anything below 84 points doesn't make it onto a shelf.

02

Roast

Batches stay under 20kg on our drum roaster, profiled per origin to bring out the lot's own character rather than a house flavor.

03

Cup

Every roast is tasted again before release, and again seven days later, to confirm it peaked the way we planned.
