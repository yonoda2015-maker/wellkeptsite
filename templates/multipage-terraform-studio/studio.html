About

A landscape practice with mud on its boots.

Terraform Studio is nineteen people across Portland and Sapporo. We stay small on purpose: every project gets a principal on site, not just on the title block.

Principles

Four rules we don't break

  1. Hydrology before geometry

    The drainage plan is the design. If the water diagram doesn't work, the prettiest planting plan is a future maintenance ticket.

  2. Design for year ten, not opening day

    We specify plantings for what they become, budget for establishment care, and write maintenance manuals the grounds crew can actually use.

  3. Local material, local labor

    Stone from within 200 miles, plants from regional nurseries, and contractors who will still be nearby when the warranty period ends.

  4. The public is the client

    Committees sign our contracts, but the people who cut through the site at 7 a.m. are who we design for. We prototype with plywood and ask them.
