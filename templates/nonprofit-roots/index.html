How we grow

Land Reclaim

We negotiate directly with cities and landowners to turn vacant lots into legally protected garden space.

Grow Kits

Every new garden gets free soil remediation, raised beds, tools, and a season of seedlings.

Youth Program

After-school garden education for 6-14 year olds in every ROOTS neighborhood.
