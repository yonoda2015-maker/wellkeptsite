Before you ask

I don't know what my site is built on — can you still help?

Yes. Send us the URL and we'll check for you, free, before you sign up for anything. Most small business sites run on WordPress; if yours doesn't, we'll tell you what we found and what your options are.

Do I need to get on a call?

No. Everything — the audit, onboarding, and monthly reports — happens over email. If you'd rather talk something through, email works better for us too: it gives you an exact written record of what we found and fixed.

Do I need to change hosting or move my site?

No. We work with your site exactly where it lives now. You just add us as an administrator, and we take it from there.

What if something breaks anyway?

Because we back up daily, we can roll your site back to the last working version — usually within the hour, at no extra cost.

Is there a contract?

No. Month to month, cancel any time from your account with no cancellation fee.
